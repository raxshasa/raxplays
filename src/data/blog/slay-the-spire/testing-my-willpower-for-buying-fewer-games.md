I've got many, many hours in Slay the Spire across multiple platforms and playing it now really is a case of trying not to buy the sequel. It looks awesome and I know I'll buy it at some point, but ideally I'd wait a _little_ bit longer. Playing the first one is a case of trying to scratch that itch while not making the impulse worse...

## Ascension

Slay the Spire is a tough game. It can be pretty punishing if you're not paying attention. I've had a long-held desire to try to get to ascension 20 in all of the classes, but realistically will settle for any of them at this point. I got to about ascension 10 on most of them on the Switch, but on Steam I'm only at one or two for most classes.

And I'm going for the Silent at the moment. My preferred build there is a defensive poison build but I'm very much of the opinion that you need to deal with whatever cards you get in the first few encounters and build from there. It's not always that clean.

My runs, however, were pretty terrible this evening. I lost a lot and pretty early, only getting to Act 3 once. It feels like I've lost some of the muscle memory that I used to have for this game, despite it not being reactions based... That's not to say I didn't have a lovely time, but it was frustrating to lose a couple of runs that felt like they were shaping up nicely, only to hit hard blocking bosses.

Even so, I can sense that Slay the Spire is pulling my attention on Steam Deck, so Mewgenics is probably back-seated for now, perhaps unfairly.
