There really aren't that many cross-platform games that support more than four players out there. Hence, I'm back with Rocket League. 

I don't think Rocket League is a bad game. Minute to minute it is fast, fun and explosive. It does a great job of building tension and is surprisingly friendly to new players, although the skill season is massive.

## Flat tyres

So why don't I feel more positive about it? I enjoy it while I play: I'm aware that some of that might be that I'm playing with folks I know and that is fun by itself. 

I think that while an individual game is a lot of fun, I generally find the whole thing a little samey. I'm not the best at it, not the worst, and I do feel that I'm getting a little better each time. But I don't care. It doesn't do a fanstastic job at five players or incentivising multiple matches. There didn't seem to be a mini-league table or similar that we could work towards. Perhaps I simply don't care as the social aspect is the primary driver for me and the rest of is merely secondary. Who knows.

I'm sure if we had been able to play competitive, that might feel different. Perhaps it's that much more spicy with real humans involved. But I'm not even sure there's a 5v5 mode, so we might well have struggled anyway.

## More than just Soccar?

Part of the "samey" feeling might come from sticking to the default game mode. While we were in a private match, we could have explored other options like Rumble, which adds random power-ups, or Hoops, which replaces the goals with basketball hoops. I wonder if dipping into these would have broken up the monotony and added a different kind of chaos to the evening. It's something to consider for next time.

## Next up

Generally, however, I want more variety of titles at this player count and cross-platform. I don't really want my solution to be buying a PS5...
