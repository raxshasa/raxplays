Short session, I played only two matches, but it's starting to click for me.

## Slow down to speed up

I've been really focussing on slowing down between matches and picking through what the game is serving up far more deliberately. Yes, I'm having to consciously do that. No, that's probably still not ideal. It is, however, making a difference. I almost immediately started feeling a lot more connected with my club. I still feel like the UI is fighting a lot of this, but the sense of immersion is definitely increased.

I'm conscious that it might be that I'm getting to the pointy end of the season, which is always more gripping. But I sense it's more than that as it's causing me to think more about in between sessions, which I've not had in this edition so far. I'd tentatively move from a 6/10 to a 7/10.
