Yes, I'm admittedly quite tired, but this evening Ball x Pit has thoroughly been kicking my arse. I've chosen a few characters that I don't normally play with and I'm trying to combine balls I don't often take in order to find evolutions I've not unlocked yer.

## Surviving comparisons

I'm a big fan of Vampire Survivors. However that game is quite an easy one: once you get a semblance of a build together, you can quite easily go God mode and rip through a run. Ball x Pit isn't that. It feels to me like a harder title that demands more thought about your positioning and more deference to the choices that you've made.

Additionally, while there are synergies to be had, there are fewer "must pick" combos, meaning you have more scope to spec into things that you find interesting. Generally, this is a good thing and I enjoy that it's not a game where you can bite off big chunks of progress in every session.
