I feel quite odd about Ball x Pit, the oddly named addiction roguelike with, I assume, an insane premise about why you're firing balls down a corridor.

The minuute-to-minute ball slinging gameplay is undeniably compelling and the character variations makes for deep, strategic choices at each turn, subtly mixing up the decision space based on who you are and what you get offered. This aspect of the game is terrific. The between times, however, is... inexplicable. It feels like such as miss that I'm tempted to bring the whole score down to that level, which somewhere around a 5.0: it's a testament to the good part that I still consider it well.

I can get into the good another time, but for now I'm going to focus on that bad.

## Trouble at home

To say I've paid zero attention to the "story" of Ball x Pit would be a wild understatement. But whether I had or not, the between run section of the game is flat out bad. There is no narrative framing in the world that would make this an interesting puzzle. It's one in which you plop buildings into an expandable play space and then fire your characters at them to generate resources and other things in order to generate more resources. Many of these buildings are passive buffs to some or all characters. You need to hit them several times in order to build or upgrade them.

But that's kind of it. I'm willing to be proved wrong and this in fact becomes an interesting problem later into the game, but for now it offers no challenge, no tangible reward and little to no satisfaction. It's so hard to care about, it's unreal. The resources themselves are traditional things like food, stone and wood, but I still don't _really_ know why I need them. There are quantities of them required for each building, but they seem to be so abundant that I again don't care.

There is no problem space here. No choice between multiple buildings where I can only afford one. No forward planning in order to try to do things efficiently. I might unlock a single new building during a run. Due to my massive resource numbers I plop it right away. I aim all my dudes at it. Done. I honestly feel like I'm "doing it wrong".

Anyway, this was a short session and I enjoyed getting back into the game. I feel a bit like I'm casting around for a sticky title at the moment, which is made harder by not having many solid sessions at the moment!
