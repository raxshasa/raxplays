Transport Fever 2 is one of those games that I just like regardless of its flaws. I'm super pumped for the next edition, so played some of the current version to whet the whistle. To be honest, I was a little bit bummed out when Good Shepherd announced that they would be partnering with Paradox to publish the third title.

## Flawed

There are issues with Transport Fever 2. Town development is limited. Maps are fine, but it doesn't take long to "mine" all of the things you want to and not feel like there's a lot left to do. Mods help with this and keep things more interesting. Ultimately, it's a _very_ nice train simulator and that's one of the main things I'm after.

## Sequels

I love what is being shown on the third installment in the series. It feels like they are addressing a lot of the things that I'm generally not thrilled about. It's definitely one for my wishlist.
