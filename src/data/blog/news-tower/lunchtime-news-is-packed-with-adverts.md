A short lunchtime hit and I've been thinking about News Tower so fired it up. The way it handles feature drip is really very good.

## Maptime

There's a map in News Tower. At the start of a week you choose a district of New York that you're looking to impress and you need to print stories that match what that district likes to up your readship there. Once you "complete" a district you can move further through the node graph, expanding your reach and influence. In each district there are key buildings that offer a variety of things. These act as progression gates.

For instance, during this session I headed north and opened up the "ad agency", which gives me an option of a whole new revenue stream. I can now install a sales desk, employ an advertiser and write up, typeset and then arrange adverts in my paper! This uses much of the UI of the regular stories, so the flow is very familiar when you get there, but it offers a choice in terms of the content you produce.

The typesetting and arrangement utilises the same personnel that your stories flow through, so you could end up gumming up the works with too many ads and need to balance things. I've not actually printed a paper with an advert in yet, but I'm looking forward to seeing the revenue gains it offers.

What struck me, however, is how elegant a way this is to introduce a new feature. It makes use of the map functionality, which was there already and, as noted, much of the rest of the existing news production process, all while offering a new option and choice for the player. This is excellent design, as it tickles the right centres of the brain while not overburdening with more cognitive load.

I'm really liking what this title is offering. It's a very tight and coherent experience.
