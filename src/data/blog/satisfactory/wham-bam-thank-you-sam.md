Some fixes, some exploring and finally... SAM. It's taken a while but I'm on the way to dimension depots!

## Power problems

Power in Satisfactory is likely one of those things that some people are going to like and some people are going to properly _hate_. It's easy enough to check how things are shaping up - all power poles and power producing machines will tell you - but basically you need to be aware of your capacity, your current consumption and your maximum consumption. Flying to close to the max consumption is dangerous (it fluctuates as machines back up and then turn themselves off) and if your current consumption goes over capacity - boom! a fuse blows.

It's easy enough to flip power back on. You can again do that from any power pole, but if you fundamentally aren't producing enough power it's going to blow again.

Early on, blowing fuses is pretty common. You likely got enough self sustaining power up to help smooth things out and then went off and built more stuff. A coal plant generates 75MW of power and a constructor only uses 4MW, so depending on the build you can likely do quite a lot without too much redundancy. But once you pop a chain down and get a 15MW assembler on the end of it, you can quickly realise your issue.

At my current stage, it's a simple case of getting a couple more coal plants going and I'm away. I needed a little more water and a pump as a result, but it was fine. Late game, blowing a fuse where you haven't properly segmented your power grid can be a multi-hour process to get back online. I had it once: I needed to jump start my nuclear plant once I'd moved it onto its own loop and then turn on everything else incrementally. Personally I think it's lots of fun, but your mileage may vary.

I would love to know how much discussion went into the design of these systems. Inherently, this is a punishment mechanic: it serves to "slow you down" and prevent you doing other things. But it's also another logistical challenge that ties into all of the same systems that the rest of the game relies on. Late on, in the age of nukes, there's waste and radiation to deal with, too.

As with other areas of the game, lots of quality of life changes have gone into this aspect. Things like allowing biomass burners to be belt fed, when previously they were manual only. Ensuring that power generators can have power shards inserted, which linearly increases their output etc. It's interesting.

## SAMs town

Once I had a few things squared away, I went off in search of SAM again. I finally found a node right as I was getting ready to stop. Because I run power everywhere I go, I was able to hook it up right away and start producing ore. I'm going to need a little bit more in order to produce the solid state of SAM I need for the depots, but that's a problem for next time.
