I decided to start exploring the area around me, as there are still ores that I haven't uncovered. Traversal is fun.

SAM ore is something I'm going to need in order to start researching the SAM branch of the MAM. It's what is going to allow me to create the dimension depots that were so revolutionary when the game hit 1.0. In Early Access, travelling the map could be an arduous process. You always needed _something_ and would have to head back to your monolithic base in order to pick up some simple iron rods or something equally innocuous.

That all changed in 1.0 with a few tweaks.

## Age of exploration

Firstly, an early tool you can unlock, the parachute saw some changes. Initially, you would need a few of these. They were disposable, only lasting one or two uses. They were incredibly good for crossing some of the larger crevasses that the Satisfactory map offers, but their consumable nature and slightly awkward crafting requirements were not great.

In 1.0, they stopped being consumable: you only need one now. This is mega, as when combined with [the zipline I mentioned previously](thank-you-mam), you can get around really easily.

So I tooled around the map. I'm in an area I can't remember very well and I'm trying to avoid guides or pointers. It meant finding SAM ore was tough and, ultimtely, I didn't. I got more hard drives, two Somersloops and a few Mercer spheres, which is great. But boy do I want some SAM. If I don't find it next time I can sense some cheating in my future.

The dimension depots are so key because they are the last piece of the exploration puzzle. They allow you to _always_ access some of the basic materials you need a lot of, as well as upload things from your own inventory. This makes inventory management a breeze: nothing ever feels out of reach.

## Age of steel

Having failed miserably to find what I was after, I decided to get some steel production up and running. It's one of the first thing you need loads of: steel pipes and beams are a pain in the butt.
![It's still in uggo mode](src/assets/images/satisfactory/steel-plant-interior.jpg)
_It's still in uggo mode_

I was fortunate enough to find an alternate recipe that made steel ingots a little faster to produce and even more fortunate to have a place not far from my main setup with everything on tap. A quick production boost to the miner on my coal node and I have a reasomable first foundry setup going on.

It's steel that powers you through tiers 3 and 4 and - while it never becomes irrelevant - requirements drop off steeply in the late game. I generally end up building one mega-steel plant and then forgetting about it entirely.

It's also the first time I've decided to build the foundry as a proper "building". This has exposed the same issues I always have in this game and anything else with building mechanics: I'm good at building rigid, square boxes and little else.

![Yes, it's a square box](src/assets/images/satisfactory/steel-plant.jpg)
_Yep, it's a square box_

I do love popping conveyor holes in and routing things nicely, but in terms of imagination I'm sorely lacking. I also don't have any roof options yet, so it's just kind of open. Maybe I'll go jaunty with the roof.

## Next up

I'll need to do some more exploring to find some SAM ore and that will give my machines some nice time to generate the materials I need to knock out a few of the tiers in the Hub. That's the other nice thing about exploration: you come how to surplus.
