Killing time in Satisfactory is rarely a problem. I assume the devs designed around this, in fact, in order to make it appealing. I'm killing time.

It's no bad thing. I still have a bajillion Smart Plates to make. Everything else has finished except this item. I made some crude hacks to double the output of that chain in order to bring the delivery time down from three hours to just over one.

But either way, I didn't fancy exploring, so it was time to build.

## Rotor Tower

I tend to think of the factories I build as relatively self contained. There are occasions where there's a few nearby developments that need a load of screws or something, but in general I manufacture according to need on site.

Rotor Tower is going to be the first of my low tier item generators. I think of materials in four levels: basics, intermediate, complex, high-end and - surprise minor fifth category - omfghowmanyofthesedoineed. Those only appear in the late game.

Rotors are a classic intermediate item and pretty much the first of that category. They combine a few of the basics and it's wholly iron based, unless you have alt recipes available (which I don't)

So I spend some time building a monolith in service to rotors.

!["It's pretty ugly at the moment"](src/assets/images/satisfactory/sand-citys-first-tower.jpg)
_It's pretty ugly at the moment_

I decided that this was going to be narrow and tall. I have a tendency to make quite wide, blocky structures that all end up looking pretty similar, so only going 6 units wide was something a little different for me. I'm also going to aim to make everything walkable. I'm not goood enough at planning so you're always going to need to jump pver belts, but I'm at least going to make stairs for each floor. It's actually pretty handy for getting up and down to build, pre-hoverpack.

It's fun getting back into building to spec and it's not something I've been doing much of. There are a few places where I've done it to small degrees, such as my steel manufacturing, but mostly I've been slapping things down and not thinking about it.

So to be actually putting up walls and organising things has been fun. It's immediately made me realise that I need to tear down pretty much everything else, at least in the immediate vicinity. I've been filling up bins of items and all of it is very haphazard, so some order is needed in the chaos.

!["The view from the tower is also ugly..."](src/assets/images/satisfactory/view-from-the-tower.jpg)
_The view from the tower is also ugly..._

I got most of the way through the parts I need, which start as steel rods, some of which gets broken down into screws and then recombined into rotors. Once I put the top floor in, I'll be producing way more of an item that is a real pain the arse.

## Next up

I'm keen to finish Rotor Tower, but I'm aware I want a few more of the cosmetic items from the AWESOME shop before I get very much older. Otherwise I tend to leave things with the notion that I'll come back to them, which I never do.
