The early game is so much longer than I remember it being! Getting power sorted is reassuring, but there's an avalanche of modular frames coming my way...

## The many stages of power automation

I unlocked the possibility of coal power at the end of my last session and at the start of this one I remembered part of the reason I love this title. I need to build a coal plant. That's fine. On building it, I remembered that coal plants need water. In the last tier I also unlocked a water extractor.

On legging it over to the nearby lake to place a water extractor, I realised that I was going to need copper sheeting to place it. This is _also_ something that I unlocked.

![My first coal plant in action](src/assets/images/satisfactory/automated-power.jpg)
_My first coal plant in action_

So I head to a new copper node and set up the miner > smelter > constructor setup that I was going to need for sheeting. I popped a storage container on the end of it, as I'm going to need plenty of these. Eventually I'm able to plop down what I need, but I only have the materials for one plant. This is fine for now as it adds two and a half times the power uplift of a new burner and needs no looking after. Lovely. My biofuel burning is giving me enough overhead for now, so I move on to other things.

## Screws and iron and screws and plates and screws

There's a bit of a joke in the Satisfactory community that screws are the bane of everyone's existance. You need _so many_ of them and often at throughputs that are uncomfortable at early belt speeds. I also don't have the alternate recipes that help mitigate the number you need.

I therefore go out to find some more nodes to start pushing out screws. It's the first time I need to pop down foundations in order to organise things a little better. I'm going to need to setup a manifold for this.

Again, this is one of those things I love about the game. A manifold is a set of machines with belts splitting bewteen them, with quantities roughly balanced.

I didn't quite get as far as I would have liked. I need reinforced plates in order to get to frames. I haven't quite got my quantities and setup right, meaning it's messier than I'd have liked. I'm going to tear all of this down soon anyway, so it's not a big deal, but it's taking me a while to get back into the swing of the aesthetics!

Anyway, I should be able to pop it all together next time, where I'll be able to move on to the next tier.
