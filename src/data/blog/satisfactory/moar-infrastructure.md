I'd forgotten what a slippery slope this game is.

Early on it's hard to get power to be self-sustaining. You're reliant initially on leaves and wood before you're able to get to solid biofuel.

## Power problems

Rushing to the chainsaw allows me to find one of the few places near me with trees and go to town on them. Picking up wood manually is sloooow and not really worth it, but it's a way faster material for creating biofuel. The chainsaw is key for this, it clears trees very fast and generates tons of wood. It's then a simple enough case to automate creating biomass and then solid biomass to power burners. Fill up a storage container with a few hundred stacks of wood lasts for a long time and allows you to effectively forget.

This is one of the very nice quality of life changes that Coffee Stain made from Early Access to launch. Previously, Biomass Burners didn't have an input. You had to keep them manually fed. That meant that banks of burners wasn't especially effective as they were too time consuming. The addition of an input means you're able to set up plenty of burners and keep your machines running for longer.

In turn, you can last and grow a lot longer without coal - the first self-sustaining power option - being a necessity. In fact, in my first playthrough I kept my biomass plant up and available the whole time. It was a fallback option if a fuse blew and I was struggling to get the grid back online.
