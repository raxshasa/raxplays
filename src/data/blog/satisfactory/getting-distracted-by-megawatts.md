I had many plans for this session to start "Sand City", which I was intending to be my first proper setup. I got waylaid. "Sand City" was likely to be a series of smaller factories, formalising the production of most of the more basic parts, which should be fine in the area I'm in. I name my sections like this to denote the area of the map they are in. I end up creating a few of these cities, which collect resources from the area, before settling on one to develop out further into my ultimate hub.

## Power drain

But I hit some snags very early. A couple of minutes into loading up my save, a fuse blew. I banged in a couple more coal plants but found them blowing again. I think there are a couple of reasons.

Firstly, I've not set the plants up very well. I'm not really balancing coal or water delivery and haven't laid things out neatly. Also, as I'm now sending more things to depots, I have more machines coming online more often. I'm getting spikier power demands. This is a bad combo. It also meant I had trouble getting things back online. Given the spider web of power cables I've left around, I couldn't bring my power generation online very well without blowing everything because I couldn't isolate it. It made getting more power in quite tough.

After fiddling with my existing power setup for a while, I decided to cut my losses, leave power off for a bit and solve it more properly. I found a couple of good coal nodes not too far away and relatively near a vital water supply. It was time for a more properly planned out plant.

It took at little over an hour - pipes were giving me grief - but I eventually got eight more coal plants online in an incredibly stable manner. I jump started them with biomass burners and was away. As part of this effort I also unlocked advanced power distribution, giving me access to power towers. With these bigger, long range options available I'll be able to make sure I can more easily cut off the rest of the network if I blow a fuse again: I can get the power running and then start to turn things on in stages. I'll need to back this up by making sure I disconnect some of the smaller parts of the web to isolate various areas, but it'll be worth it.

## Pipe problems

This did highlight one of the areas of the game that I find most frustrating: fluid dynamics. I get that they are setup in a way that makes you think about them, but the main reason I find myself thinking of them is how annoying they are. Stopping "sloshing" is a big pain and getting pumps both in the right place and functioning properly can, on occasion, feel quite random.

Ultimately, I like the way pipes look and the differing function they offer, but they are one of the most brittle things in the game.

## Steel ramp up

I finished the session by adding a floor to my steelworks to bring encased beams into play. They are going to be something I need lots of and my current effort is wildly insufficient in the long term, but they will do me for now. With some beams in hand I can start thinking about getting some larger infrastructure in place.

## Next up

I mentioned I was hoping to get properly in to Mewgenics, and I am, but at my PC, now is not that time. I am very much still in the grip of this title.
