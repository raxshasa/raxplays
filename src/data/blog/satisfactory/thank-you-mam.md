Research started in earnest. Caterium was a reasonable distance from my starting point and I need to research through the caterium branch in order to unlock the zipline. The MAM is the "workbench" required for that.

## Zipping around

Movement is pretty fast in Satisfactory, but distances are also pretty big.

The zipline is one of your first traversal options. It makes use of your existing infrastructure as you zoom along on your power lines. In fact, it encourages you to run power lines all over the place. They are cheap and fast to build: the fact that they then double as a highway of sorts is fantastic.

There are poles in the way of course, but you can jump from wire to wire. The movement speed of the zip is faster than running and it respects downhills more than it respects uphills, so you can move around really quite fast.

Again, one of the things I love about this title: the ziplines remains relevant. Yes, it's one of the first tools at your disposal for moving around. It's also one that you're likely to rely on for pretty much your entire playthrough. The jetpack and hoverpack are both better traversal tools, but the zipline has its own utility. If you're falling, you can hold the button for the zip and it'll latch on to nearby wires as you go past them. This will prevent fall damage and, often, is in fact your best way of getting around. It doesn't need fuel like the jetpack and it's faster than the hoverpack.

It's quite a testament to the design that a tool like this, something that would be superseded in other titles, can remain so relevant throughout the whole run.

It sure is good to be back.
