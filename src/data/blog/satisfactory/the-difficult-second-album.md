I've just hit a point I remember from the first game, where there's almost too many things I could be doing and they are all quite far apart. I'm suffering from choice paralysis

## Trekking back and forth

For this playthough I decided to start in the advanced starting position. There are more hostiles and fewer convenient starting nodes. As a new player this would be quite tough, but in general I haven't personally found it to be super different, other than involving me spending more time in terrain I was much less familiar with.

But I've hit a point in my progression and in the research tree where I'm not quite going for the second phase material delivery, but I'm very much wanting to expand operations. There are simply too many things I want to do:

1. Want to get a source of sulphur coming in somewhere convenient
2. Need quartz in order to make silica and start making crystal components
3. Want more SAM for fluctuators
4. Going to need the next round of machines in order to produce more advanced goods
5. Need to solve my occasional plates / screws bottlenecks

All of these things need doing and parts of them overlap. I therefore start something, get distracted by something else, start a third thing, decide to switch gears, have my eye caught by something on the horizon etc etc.

It's odd, because inherently this is the sort of thing I _should_, personally, find frustrating. And sometimes I kind of do. I'm frustrated with myself that I'm not able to focus on one thing or forget why I was going to X place to begin with. Generally, however, because the thing I've been distracted by is fun in its own right or benefits me in some other way, it's not so bad.

In this session, I basically progressed all of those 5 things to some extent without finishing any of them. That sounds like it could be unfulfilling or feel like lost or slow progress, but it's not. I like it. Not as much as "finishing" something, but for the deadtime inbetween larger projects, it's great. I [mentioned before](the-steel-age) that one of the beauties of the game is that if I slow down on something or get distracted, it just means my store of materials elsewhere is likely filling up, giving my a valuable surplus to utilise.

## Next up

I'm feeling the need to break up my _Satisfactory_ sessions, especially as I want to [give _Mewgenics_ more of a try](../games/mewgenics), so perhaps I'll put this down for a week or so. Famous last words.
