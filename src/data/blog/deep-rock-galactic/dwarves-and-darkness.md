Not something I've played for a long while, but I saw that Rogue Core is coming out soon and that's definitely something I'm interested in. My love for Deep Rock is deep and abiding. I think it's _so_ well made and just a good time. It's a shame that it's not cross-play at all, as it really limits it as a title that's easy to play with friends.

## Tie ins

It's an easy win for the team at Coffee Stain to launch a little content update in advance of the early access release of Rogue Core, the new spinoff from the main title. Getting people dropping in again is just what they want to achieve and, with me at least, it has worked. I never would have played this evening if not for seeing that update. It got me in the mood.

As ever, Deep Rock is a blast. It's been ages since I played and I was hardly a serious miner even when I did play more, but even so it's never daunting to team up with randos. Even though I was getting downed _preeeetty_ regularly, it doesn't seem that people complain. There are no accusations in the group chat. No snark. People just pop you back up again and you keep on trucking.

Can't wait for the new title. Rock and stone!
