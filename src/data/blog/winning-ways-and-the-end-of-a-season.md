OK so technically I didn't quite _finish_ the season, but I got pretty close. I won the league with five games to spare, so it was pretty handy in the end. I have thoughts.

## Finishing and an actual new feature
Generally, if the season isn't going down to the last day, I find the run in to the end of a winning season really tedious. Obviously cup semis or finals are different, but dead rubber league games stink. 

This new edition does have one shiny new feature that makes this better! Instant result. It's oddly tucked away in settings, but it's great. You set up the team, do the team talk, start the match and then hit isntant result. You then make a choice that I don't like and it plays out the match.

The choice I don't like is around how to approach the match. You have to choose something, there's no "use what is set up now" option. Many of them make sense, like "strangle the game" etc. I don't think I'd use it if things were in the balance, as I don't really trust what it would do, but I get some people might. I've been choose "play for the win", but I'm a little concerned it's going to bang on an aggressive approach and that's not really what I want. I wish there were a default that is effectively "maintain the status quo, making small opposition adjustments as you suggest and make some subs". Although perhaps a little more succintly. 

Generally though, I like it. Previously you could go on holiday to skip this stuff but that's not quite what I was after. That technically put your assistant in charge, which sucks, and the simulation engine was different to the match engine. This was a necessity, it's the same simulation engine used to process results that aren't your own, but it means it makes mistakes. 

This new instant result option - I assume - is simply ticking the match engine at mega-speed to get a "real" result in the match engine proper. I could be totally wrong and it's all nonsense. Who knows.

## Winning on the pitch
So I've achieved automatic promotion and I'm generally starting to feel better about the game. The real proof will, I suspect, manifest at the start of the new season. It's always a tough time: boring things like contract renewals, fun things like trying to stretch my meagre wage budget to some decent freebie players. Pre-season friendies, which I delegate immediately. 

I'm looking forward to it. I'm wondering if my recent positive vibes are due to winning + end of season + getting a golden generation of youth intake coming in. Time will tell.
