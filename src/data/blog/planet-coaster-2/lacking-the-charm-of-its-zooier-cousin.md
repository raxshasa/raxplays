I've tried a few times to get in to this one and I just can't quite bring myself to love it, or even really like it. And maybe I do? But I also feel like I don't. I very much want to, as the Planet series has a charm that I find quite irresistable, but in this instance I'm left a little cold.

## Devil in the details

In Planet Zoo, a game for which I own about a zillion DLCs, I will spend a long time refining habitats, making them look natural and lived in. I'll design social spaces for guests, detailing little bits and pieces here and there. The path system is a bit of a disgrace, but I love the title dearly.

Some of that - a lot of that in fact - is missing for me in Coaster. Yes, I have less content and my fingertips as I'm DLC-lite in comparison, but I don't have the same desire to make things good as I do in Zoo. Ironically, the path system is much, _much_ better, but it still brings with it frustrations.

I'm not _quite_ able to do everything I want to in terms of routing paths in interesting ways around the scenery I want to build. Trying to build a functional two-storey saloon, for instance, is a lesson in frustration. It would be in Planet Zoo, too, but there I'm less inclined to try it, as it's less "zoo" to do that kind of thing.

Here in Planet Coaster, the theming feels like it ought to be more "wacky" and theme park-y. So it's therefore that much more frustrating.

## Subject matters

In Planet Zoo, the fact that I'm making habitats and viewing platforms etc for a host of adorable animals really helps. Part of my struggle with Planet Coaster is that outside of building and designing the titular coasters, I'm not that interested in the rides. The amount of customisation you can add is wonderful and varied, but some of the ride pod designing feels incredibly arduous. Partly this is a UI issue: finding the right scenery parts I want to use to decorate pods is a slog, as search and filtering are OK-ish, but not quite strong enough to make it appealing. I'm therefore mostly designing guest facilities and, as mentioned above, I struggle to get enthusiastic about it.

There are also some manjor annoyances for me around how the ride maintenance pans out. I'm sure there are settings I can play with, but out of the box I find the mechanics to be incredibly inefficient and therefore annoying.

## Next steps

Ultimately, it's the fact that I feel like it takes a long time - too long - to make anything look good that leaves me a little cold. I might well come back to this and try again, but for now I'm going to carry on getting pumped for [Planet Zoo 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3219030/Planet_Zoo_2/), which is basically a day 1 purchase for me.
