## Boss fights

The bosses in the first title were hard as nails. Proper bastards. They were generally fun, but proper run enders if it went wrong. In hindsight, with the slightly more random nature of the combat, they could be punishing in a slightly unfair way. I've only now fought one boss in the second title, but I like what I've seen.

The design of the boss was as lovely as in the the first title. Interesting mechanics as well as a slightly gruesome vibe. He was also, and importantly, slightly more predictable and slightly easier to fight. It seems like bosses appear at the end of "lairs", which require you to fight through two rooms prior to the boss. They are therefore seeing you at your most vulnerable.

If the game retains this approach to boss fights, I'll be happy.

## Inventory blight

You pick up a lot of items in Darkest Dungeon. From fight rewards to crashing through barriers in your stagecoach, there's plenty to pick up. There is an inventory cap in the game and... I hate it.

The main reason I don't like it is that it does not seem to have a purpose. Inventories in most games, like action / adventure games, are there for "realism" purposes. They stop your character being able to carry everything under the sun, which is immersion breaking.

However in Darkest Dungeon, I just don't see the point. You're on a stagecoach for a start, so presumably you can pack plenty in. I'm sure it's possible to upgrade the stagecoach to carry more, but the limit is quite low at the moment.

And I don't see the positioning of it. It isn't an interesting thing to puzzle out - you just have to discard things. It's hard to compare certain items with one another in the UI, making it a tedious experiene. Finally, it's also based on item count, rather than somthing like weight, so it doesn't even feel properly thought out. It's a shame.

## Next steps

I did a couple of runs this evening. I'm still positive about the game, although picking up a run mid-way through after a couple of weeks is very hard to to.

I'm also still finding some of the UI quite obtuse.

I did unlock a couple more characters in my first run and took them out in the second. They are all well done: plenty of character is packed in to the various classes and it's fun to play with the team composition as a result. I get the feel that as I play more I'm going to find more synergies that I want to explore. That's exciting.
