Early Access is still a good place for this title, which continues to look absolutely lovely. The aestehtics here have always been absolutely lovely and that's still true. It's a uniquely relaxing town builder, which you should absolutely play on slower speeds as much as possible.

## Gameplay

This was a pretty short session, as I just had a hankering to fire it up during a lunchtime and it is currently installed on my mechanical drive. As ever, I'm somewhat floored by the vibes here, with the assets, lighting and art style delivering a powerhouse of an experience. The developer has clearly brought a new UI artist on board, as the UI is sublime, with illustrated cards and inforamtion everywhere. It's best in class stuff.

Otherwise, the core loop remains nice. There's something here that feels a little brittle or shallow, but I think a lot of that is still because of the early game nature of where I'm getting to. There is - on the face of it - more content here than I've seen since the last time I played. It looks like town development is a little more nuanced and there's more life on the map, but I've not run in to that just yet.

## Next up

I don't think I'll pick too much of Manor Lords back up too soon, but it was nice to see some development of the content and systems since the last time I fired it up.
