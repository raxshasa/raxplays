So now I've [_actually_ finished the season](winning-ways-and-the-end-of-a-season), it's time to roll on into a brand new league. Having had The Message, that I'm seeing a golden generation in the youth team, I'm pretty excited.

## Domestic double

OK so the cup was the FA Trophy (amusingly abbreviated to FAT by the game) but still, Woking haven't won it since 1997 so I'm happy. The match itself was aginst Halifax, who happened to be my final league fixture of the season, too. I won that league game 6-3, in a match where neither defence covered themselves in glory.

In contrast, the final was a tight and unambitious affair. I snatched a goal in the last 20 minutes to take the win.

The game handled the win... OK. There was some incredibly odd media stuff - as there often is - where they asked me if I had been worried about the match, given my shaky recent form. Having lost one game of the last 22, this was a surprising question but I think I handled it well. The trophy presentation was nice.

## Youth is our future

Far more excitingly, I got my youth intake through, who my staff see as a golden generation. Development of young players is one of my favourite things in the entire series, so this was an awesome one to get.

Again, the game handled this...OK. Of the five (FIVE!) "elite" talents I've supposedly got, four of them have got _identical_ portraits and the fifth has the same face but slightly different hair. That's a shame. The faces aren't great and it doesn't make a huge difference to me personally, but I can see how this somewhat dents things for a lot of people. It's a shame that it feels like yet another barrier to immersion.

## Next up

In any case, this was only a short session to properly finish off the season by playing the cup final. I'm looking forward to seeing how things go in League Two next year.
