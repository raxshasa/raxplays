Big Walk is something that has been on my radar for a little while and I've been getting more hyped for. It's an intriguing prospect. I have plenty to mull over mechanically on this one, but initially I want to look at the vibes and position of this title.

## Slop?

Friendslop is a term that is thrown around a lot. It has become incredibly prominent in recent times, with the rise of titles that cater to groups of people who want to have fun in a wide variety of ways. You could, un-generously, put _Big Walk_ in that category.

However, you would be wrong to do so. There are many of the signs here: proximity chat, high player count, drop-in-drop-out play etc etc. But there's far, far more to it than that.

One of the big differences is that this isn't a short loop of a game that you repeat to see what hilarity will ensure and nor is it a roguelike in any way, shape or form. It's a linear, curated puzzle adventure that has an end and is intended to be played with a group from start to finish. It'll adapt to different player counts, but inherently it's a deliberately designed experience.

Aspects such as the aforementioned pirximity chat are instead used to incredible effect to enhance the overall vibe of the game. And that vibe is one of chill, contemplation and teamwork. You are given a wide range of charming tools, the details of which I'll cover another time, and need to make use of those to achieve your goals. It's fair to say that our group was pretty hooked by what is on offer to begin with, and the end of the latest session led to some reveals that certainly whet the appetite for the next session.

## Next up

I can't wait to get back to this and it's testament to the strong early impressions that there are _seven_ of us looking to get together to carry on. This has never happened. I've rated this at an 8 and fully expect to be upping that...
