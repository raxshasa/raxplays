It has been a solid 25 years or so since I played this game last time and OH BOY, do I not remember it being this tough. I do think games are getting "more accessible" (read: easier) across the board in general, but this one feels particularly punishing.

## Control scheming

Part of it, I think, is the controls. The handling of the ships is _pretty_ sensitive and it's hard to stay on target (lol) when tracking enemies. 

This is especially true of faster moving enemies. Those things are hard to get a hold of.

Additionally, the speed at which these ships move and pivot in general is high, and therefore hard to handle. The UI doesn't do much to help. There's a minimap, but it doesn't show verticality. There are no other onscreen, more immediate indicators as to where enemies are, it's all on the minimap or picking up your visual scanning.

It all adds up to me failing to break into the silver or gold tiers of the scoring per missions, instead earning a bronze or - not unlikely - absolutely nothing... Not to mention plenty of fails.

## Charm and atmosphere

With that, however, comes a lovingly made game that delivers a lot of fun. The ship models themselves are well made and the overall soundscape is excellent. From the little whistles and beeps when navigating the 3D menu through to the dialogue throughout missions, a lot of the space on the cartridge must be taken up by audio assets.

It results in a package that feels well made and true to the source material, even given the limitations of the console when it comes to terrain and draw distance. 

## Next up

Despite how tough it is, I enjoy the title and will continue to dip into it.
