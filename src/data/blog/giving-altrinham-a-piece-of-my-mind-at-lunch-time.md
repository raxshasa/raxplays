Ah, Football Manager 26. A game I waited for with baited breath. A game that I was desperate to get stuck in to, especially after the delay. I was relying on you to carry me through the last few months of 2025. How not quite there you are.

## Short session
As a super short lunch session, I never expect much. Football Manager is a good one as you can judge pretty easily when to cut it off. If there's only a few minutes before you need to get back to it, don't start a match! Simple. 

I continue to not know how to feel about the latest installment. I thought 24 was _great_ and played it a lot. I was ready for 26, having had quite a break from the old game. 

I definitely don't get all of the hate for it. I quite like the change to the UI, even though there were (and remain) some unforgivable bugs in there. It's getting better, but it's not perfect. Generally though, I like the vibes.

But the game falls down on the feel of thigns. The match engine is pretty fantastic, as is the new tactics setup. Those two things alone make it very hard to go back to 24. Between matches, however, it's hard to feel "involved" in the running of the club. In my opinion, it's a subtle mix of some weird options being hidden in the UI and an odd top navigation bar.

There are several items in the top nav:
- Portal
- Squad
- Recruitment
- Match Day
- Club
- Career

The inclusion of Career in those options remains _inexplicable_ to me. I chcek out my own profile roughly ever other season and there's not a lot else under that menu. Even Club has almost nothing of interest in there. On the face of it it's an incredibly basic overview of things, and you're always going to be after something more specific. Realistically, you don't review Tactics _that_ often, unless there are issues in the team.

It feels like Match Day should be cool, but the weird layout means it's a horrible way to select your squad. In this section they should highlight, and make more of, the team dynamics stuff, which seems to have taken a back seat. I end up just waiting until I'm forced to select a team pre-game. You therefore spend 95% of your time in Portal, as that's just linking you to *everythying* else. It ends up feeling impersonal.

And speaking of matches, they sure do creep up on you. In previous versions you'd get a lot of persistent, on-screen reminders of who you're playing and when. Now, it's a case of waiting for the pre-game emails and then - BAM - you're choosing your midfield before you know it. Very little fanfare. It's possible this is exacerbated by the fact I'm playing lower league: there's just less fanfare in general.

Perhaps I need to spend more time in Squad. I can see how some options in there would be cool. I just added Fixtures as a bookmark, too. Let's see how we get on.
