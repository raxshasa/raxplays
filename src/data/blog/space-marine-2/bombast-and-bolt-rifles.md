This game is very, very stupid and also very, very fun. Games can be subtle. Games can be nuanced. They can make you think. _Space Marine 2_, very deliberately, does absolutely none of that.

I'm around 6 hours in now and forming a much better opinion of what's on offer here.

## Schlocky

_Space Marine 2_ is schlocky in all of the best ways. The writing is hilariously dry and full of 40k-isms that few will properly understand. These baffling lines are delivering with deadpan monotony and no shred of irony at all. It doesn't even try to explain the batshit nature of the organisation that is the Ultramarines (or any Space Marine chapter) and nor should it. There's a dude who seems to maybe be in charge but is a priest but has a skull for a helmet? Awesome. I love it.

![He's a chaplain and I have no idea what he actually does but he's important](src/assets/images/space-marine-2/chaplain.jpg)
_He's a chaplain and I have no idea what he actually does but he's important_

Now I know _enough_ about 40k lore that I understand some or even most of it, but wow is it fun to see it all in action and think about what it could possibly mean.

![The cutscenes do look very pretty](src/assets/images/space-marine-2/thunderhawks-roll-out.jpg)
_The cutscenes do look very pretty_

It's also stacks of fun. The presentation of everything is lovely and solid. The marines move with heft and weight and their animations are snappy and violent. The levels of gore are at the sort of comical level that this IP requires and the sheer number of enemies on screen speaks to some clever technical optimisations and techniques at play.

I mentioned the dynamic loadouts last time around and those really do accentuate the whole experience. Weapons are fun to play with, even if many of them are hard to remember, and I'm starting to see more of a range of extra options, too. This includes jetpacks, which lead to fast paced, interesting play with far more focus on verticality.

The story is maguffin-heavy, which in a title like this is fine by me. I'm aware of my next objective, loosely aware of how it fits into the narrative and the rest is noise. I'm only here to shoot bad guys anyway.

## Next up

I decided to play this ahead of Jedi Survivor and I'm glad of that. It's exactly the sort of mindless nonsense that I'm after at the moment. It's also much easier than Jedi is likely to be!
