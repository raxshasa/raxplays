I've been feeling pretty positive about this title, but I'm starting to feel the novelty wear off. I think I'm pretty near to the end and I'm quite glad of that.

## Realism

It's not that I'm _not_ enjoying it, I am. But I felt like there was going to be something more that transpired, mechanically. As I've noted, the plot is largely wall-to-wall maguffin to me, so I don't really have narrative to fall back on. And the combat is chunky and visceral but it's now... rote. I get it. I've "mastered" it (in that I'm terrible at it but I have a patter).

And I'm starting to get a bit bored. It's still slick, looks nice enough and holds enough enjoyment that I'll finish it, but I'm definitely feeling cooler about it than I did. I'm roughly seven hours in and I hope it's not lnoger than 10 overall.
