News Tower is a game I bought in Early Access a good old while ago and played a couple of sessions of, maybe three hours or so. I liked it a lot but it felt like one to come back to, as it was quite early in Early Access. I'm very glad I waited. 

## Evocative of the era
I will confess to falling asleep in my chair while playing this evening, but that has nothing at all to do with the game itself, which is fully delightful.

I'm still early on and so my opinions on the mechanics are still forming, but from a vibe perspective I'm very much in. From art style to actual assets to audio design, the game screams 1930s journalism. It's little things like arranging your pages based on the stories you have and then pulling the lever to start printing. This sets off a little bell that rings to signify it's starting. The camera then cuts to your printing press whirling into life while a bit of pumping music plays. It's fantastic and a great "end" to your week of writing, reporting, digging and creating.

## Next up
My early take on the full release is that they have made meaningful improvements from Early Access, some of which I started to see in my playthrough and will cover in later sessions. At the moment I'd give News Tower a 7/10.
