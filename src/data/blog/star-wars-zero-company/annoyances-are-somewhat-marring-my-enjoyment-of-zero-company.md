I'm finding this to be a super weird one. I'm fundamentally having a nice time while also getting annoyed. I've had a couple of lock ups that have required me to kill the game, losing a few turns of progress in the progress.

## Technical issues

I don't want to keep banging on about them, but the technical issues and gameplay locks are frustrating. Not only have I had two soft crashes, but I keep getting long pauses at the ends of turns. It seems like the model is stuck and the view doesn't move on: it doesn't last too long, but it's very noticable. 

## Fun to be had

However, I'm having a lot of fun with it. The loop of creating bonds, adding skills, resolving dilemmas and ultimately going on a mission is great. The missions themselves are engaging and well laid out. Level design offers plenty of scope for approaching things in a variety of ways. Elevation is fun, the various abilities are fun and the characters feel distinct and offer interesting decision spaces. I am not taking the same party each time, which I think speaks for itself. 

I noted the abilities above: I like the focus on this and not the guns. There are four guns, from what I've seen, and there are some minor attachments or utilities to add, but otherwise the gear / equipment side is minor in comparison to what your operators are actually capable of. This is far more evocative than a focus on hardware would have been.

The story seems fine, I'm skipping a lot of the incidental chatter, but trying to make sure I keep up with the narrative.

## Next up

I'm finding it pretty compelling and very much want to carry on. Next up, though, I want to mention the "exploration" sections and how egregious I find them. Keen to hit another couple of examples to make sure I'm not jumping the gun.
