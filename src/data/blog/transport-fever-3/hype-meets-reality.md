I've been HYPE for this for a while. Finally getting to get stuck into it is great. The game is very familiar and, while there are definite graphical upgrades, it doesn't actually look super different either.

However, despire there appearing to be many similarities, there's quite a lot different going on under the hood.

## Familiar trappings, new skin

IN particular, the way goods move around the map is very, very new. I need to play with it a lot more to get a full picture, but I _think_ I love it.

Goods that perish, warehouses, actual supply chain management. It all ladders up to a far more fluid experience: one that feels new and different, despite a familiar set of surroundings. I'm very much looking forward to playing more of this one.
