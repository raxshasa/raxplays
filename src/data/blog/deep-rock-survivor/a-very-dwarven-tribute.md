Somehow I've made it to almost 30 hours in this game without really realising. I've also managed to play it almost entirely mindlessly, forming very few opinions throughout.

Not because it's bad. Not at all. Mostly because it's easy: it's a game that wants you to play with with little stress.

## Stress free

And that's a little odd. Bullet heaven / hell games are inherently supposed to be stressful. But this is a fluid, flowing title that - since the UI overall on leaving Early Access - guides you through playing in a way that is absorbing.

I'm mostly putting this out there as Survivor is a _fantastic_ title on Steamdeck and I'm planning on trying to play a little more deliberately. I haven't played anything as good as Vampire Survivors since... Vampire Survivors.
