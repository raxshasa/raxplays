## Bimbling along

It's been a while since I touched Cities and I decided it was time to try to love this game again. I want to, I very badly want to, but it has so far failed to grab me in the same way as the first title.

I'm a few sessions into my current city. I'm at the stage where I should be thinking up all sorts of ideas around how and where to expand. How to improve my office districts, how best to fill them with educated folks. Where to create an old town, how to run trans and trains around it. And... I'm getting some of that. The transit options feel like they're there but I'm otherwise struggling a bit. I just don't think I care.

I'm struggling to nail down why this is. I have a couple of thoughts.

### Performance

This continues to be a massive disappointment. My PC isn't a bang out, but it's OK. I should be able to run Cities with ghosting, without stutter while panning the camera, without hitching.

Generally, I'm pretty immune to slight performance issues but here it spoils the vibes more often that I typically find. A smoother ride without making the whole game look like a smeary mess would help a lot.

### Traffic

One of the favourite parts of the first Cities was traffic managemnet. Finding smart solutions to bad intersections is big fun. Some of that exists in Cities 2 but it's inconsistent. Sometimes issues disppear and reappear, almost inexplicably. I think this is the rush hour simulation, but it's hard to tell as I turned off the day / night cycle because everything looked shit at night time.

There's no overlay that shows you where people are trying to go. I try to work it out myself and build in such a way to distribute trips, but it's hard to feel like I'm not shooting in the dark.

### Lifelessness

There's a feeling of lifelessness to the city for me. I find it hard to create commercial areas that I am excited about. I think part of this is the extreme drop in commercial demand that I find happens often. Commercial deamnd is much less consistent than any of the others. I believe this contributes to me not caring as much about the direction of my city.

## Next up

I want to carry on with my city. I'm invested _enough_ for that. Cities Skylines 2 remains below a 6/10 for me and pre-purchasing the Ultimate Edition continues to be a bad decision.
