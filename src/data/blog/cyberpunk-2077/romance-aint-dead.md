I'd kind of forgotten that romance was an option in this game, but I'm glad I saw it through. In general, I find romance in games like this to be a bit twee, a bit cringey or both. Especially with the inevitable sex scene at the end. While the second thing happened, I generally found the romance questline to be fun.

## Not Panam, but still good

There was getting to know Judy while engaging in some fun missions, which initially didn't feel like they would lead to a romance ending. I wanted to romance Panam, really, but she doesn't go for the girls. Judy also seems cool, so it's a close second.
