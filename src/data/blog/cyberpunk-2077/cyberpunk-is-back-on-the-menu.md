It's fair that Cyberpunk was a broken mess on release. It's also fair that a lot of work has gone into it since then. I dropped it shortly after launch, but was drawn back in again a couple of years later. It's good.

There are a lot of things that CDPR got wrong at launch. Some of them are still present. But taken as the game it actually is, rather than what I believe it was thougt to be, Cyberpunk is an incredible thing.

## Environmentally sublime

There are lots of things I could pick up on, but the main thing that keeps hitting me in the face is Night City itself.

Yes, for a city that is as reputationally insane as Night City is, there is suspiciously little traffic around. The pavements are also, in many areas, not bery busy. But the visuals of it all mixed with the audio and art style is incredible. There are areas of the city that feel totally oppressive in the sheer noise and hubbub that is generated. As noted, NPCs are sometimes thin on the ground so the lighting and audio effects are doing a lot of heavy lifting here.

The soundscape is bonkers, with sirens, gunfire, chatter and general _people_ noise constantly on the go. The focus that the sound designers are able to create on what you're doing is super impressive, as is the original music that plays all over the place and the way it all fades back and forth as required based on the situation. It's not something you notice unless you look for it, but it's one of those things that adds a lot of intangible atmosphere to the world.

![The city looks, to my eye, absolutely fantastic even on my feeble hardware](src/assets/images/cyberpunk/lighting-is-a-dream.jpg)

And the lighting. Oh boy is it a treat. I sacrifice some framerate to ensure I have some semblence of raytracing on, as the difference it makes to me is massive. Look at this vegetation at the front of the shot above. It's fantastic, and views like this are common in Night City. There are several "biomes" in the world, especially in the wastelands outside of the walls, but even within the city the artists are able to create some lovely differences and it's all lit superbly well.

Even if I weren't enjoying the story or the moment-to-moment gameplay, the environments alone are almost enough to keep me coming back.

![During story missions you are frequently handed beautifully arted set pieces](src/assets/images/cyberpunk/environments-are-lovely.jpg)
_During story missions you are frequently handed beautifully arted set pieces_

It has been a while since I fired up the game. I had bought the Phantom Liberty DLC after really enjoying the base game and then got distracted by other things. I'm looking forward to getting back into Cyberpunk again.
