As I feel like I'm nearing the end of Phantom Liberty, I'm mainlining the core story line and boy is it a good'un. I relatively recently felt like Odyssey for Rimworld was the best DLC I've come across in any title, but I might have to revise that opinion. Phantom Libery is _good_.

## A\* DLC

The whole experience of Phantom Liberty has felt well integrated and well produced. I dread to think how much it cost to produce. Yes, it's £25 at full price but it's now often on sale. Even at full price it feels borderline essential and getting it for less is a bargain.

The storyline and characters are far more focussed than the main game, as you'd expect from a DLC. It does a solid job of letting you hop in and out of the experience, too. Given it is all in one area, which requires you to go through a loading-screen-cunningly-disguised-as-a-checkpoint, it's able to keep the noise of the rest of Night City out of the way and give itself a unique flavour.

As with much of the rest of the main story beats, I feel the best way to experience the content is in one hit. All around, Cyberpunk feels like it wants you to tackle it in blocks of content, with some natural breaks as you "wait a day" for a character to give you a call with their next set of problems.

## Mission decision

And the missions themselves feel varied and meaningful. There are no fetch quests here, no insignificant side tracks. It's pure, hook-it-to-my-veins content, story and action. Even now, I'm in one of my favourite genre of missions in a title where I get to handle a powerful sniper rifle and guide another character through a series of lucky and coincidental areas of conflict. This is all setup to what I assume is the main meat of the final section of Phantom Liberty.

I'm interested in how they're going to tie this off. I'm assuming it's not an end-game in its own right and that it's going to tie into the base game storyline, but who knows. It's chunky enough that there could be an actual ending here.

## Next up

After I finish this sniper section, I'm going to see if I get freedom to roam again. I have decided to go all in on contagion, as I've realised there are some perks I can grab to really boost it, so I need to check how I've specced and maybe buy some upgrades before finishing this secion. I suspect it's another couple of evenings work though.
