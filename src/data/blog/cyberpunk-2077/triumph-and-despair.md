Good grief, Phantom Liberty is top tier content. It's a 10/10 knockout all by itself. I rolled credits on the DLC in this session and the ending is an absolute triumph. It's also not _technically_ the end of the content if I don't want it to be. There is more side content to complete, all of which has been pleasingly meaty so far, and there's an additional quest that exists within the base game, which I assume is a Phantom Liberty themed ending.

## Next up

I'm mighty close to upping my rating to a whopping 9.5 here. I've never played anything quite like Cyberpunk: while it neatly fits into a genre bucket, it does things that other games simply don't. Phantom Liberty is one of the best pieces of content I've ever come across. To refer to it as a "piece of content" feels like doing it a disservice. Had Cyberpunk launched in this state, I think it would be considered incredibly differently to how it is now.

While there is definitely a redemption arc here, it's still got the "yeah but launch" ball and chain tied around its ankles. I was certainly one of those people. Honestly it's a shame.
