Having rolled credits, this is likely the last of my playtime posts prior to a full review. I'm not going to say too much, as I'd prefer to save it for a review.

## Endings

Generally, however, it was the ending introduced by Phantom Liberty and I had mixed feelings about it. Initially, I didn't like it at all. It felt anticlimactic. I have come to realise that this is entirely the point of that ending. It's something I'll explore in more depth next time around.

![The view from V's apartment](src/assets/images/cyberpunk/my-view.jpg)
_The view from V's apartment_

I pretty quickly reloaded an older save that I had taken from the point of no return and kicked off a different ending instead. Part of what I didn't like much about the Phantom Liberty ending was something I knew I could rectify in one of the others. I started the initial stages of the new choice and I can tell that I'm going to vibe with it far better.

In hindsight, I do like the Phantom Liberty one from a creative standpoint. It's not immediately satisfying as a player but... that's kind of the point.

## Next up

I'm going to finish the game again and then I'll be writing a review.
