With one change in personnel, it is time for us to get back to the table and talk about some dilemmas. The format of the game is pretty similar to the first one.

## Round 1

I'm not going to go into mega detail on this session. We were establishing rules, especially for our new player. We set things up, chose our houses and starting situation and cracked on.

The core loop of the game remains sharp, interesting and well structured. It is centred in interaction and this is where the game shines. There are some new bells and whistles: more resources this time and things to manage. At the moment, the impact of those is not clear.

There is also a new track for "chronicles", which are game enhancing effects that offer more nuance to the negotiation phases. These are unlocked quite often as part of the outcomes of the dilemmas, and are often "owned" by a certain player. So far, it seems like more theme and lore to add to the rampant theming and lore throughout the rest of the game.

## Next up

As is tradition, we're going to need time between sessions in order to align diaries, so this won't be a quick one. Needless to say, however, I'm very much looking forward to the next one.
