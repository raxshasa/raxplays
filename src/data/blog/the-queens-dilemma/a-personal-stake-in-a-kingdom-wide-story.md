A second bite at this one, and it feels like it's really warming up. I'd like to look a little more at the personal touch that comes with this game.

## Round 2

In the first game, your own place in the kingdom was not particularly well rooted. For a start, each game was a generation, so your character remained a somewhat nameless representative of the house you chose.

This time, however, the timeline of the game is far shorter. You play a single character throughout and this leads to a number of effects I very much like.

Firstly, the narrative feels a little tighter. We're still really early on, but it already feels like we're getting more story, more quickly and I like that. It also feels like we're tackling 1.5 narratives, rather than several. This might be because we're still in the nascent phase of the game.

Secondly, there is far more onus on personal story. You receive more hidden quests and those are tied to your backstory and overall approach. It's possible that those are supposed to be kept secret, but we're reading them out as we achieve them, as they add a lot of flavour to the game.

My own character is a criminal underworld boss type figure, and the stories I'm looking at are unique to me and very thematic. Being able to compare that to some of the others and the types of characters they are is really good value.

## Next up

I'm loving the time we've spent with this so far. It feels so much like the first that it's very familiar, but with some extra wrinkles that help make it spicy. So far, so excellent.
