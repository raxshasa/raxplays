So while it's technically called _Vampire Crawlers: The Turbo Wildcard from Vampire Survivors_, I'm very much not going to refer to it as that... But yes, poncle has released a demo, which is a spin off from smash hit Vampire Survivors, this time as a card battler.

## Demo Dealer

Demos are a rare thing to see these days. Steam Next Fest is a fun time of year, precisely as it gives air to this forgotten practice. It's great to be able to dip a toe into some upcoming titles and - as a huge fan of Vampire Survivors - I was keen to try this out.

Aesthetically, it's identical. The art is 1:1 the same, except this time you're approaching things in the 1st person, walking around mazes battling stuff. I barely scratched the surface here, but it seems like almost all things are familiar: weapons, enemies, characters. The lot. That's pretty great, as it allowed me to carry a lot of context into the game.

Initial impressions are pretty good. It's hard to rate the game well, given my short time with it and the fact it's a demo. But it's fast, engrossing, mechanically interesting and compelling enough that I'm very pumped for the full release. I put a lot of hours into Vampire Survivors and I can immediately see that being the case again here.
