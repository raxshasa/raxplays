I hit my first boss that absolutely handed my arse to me. I've kind of been waiting for that first roguelike 'gate' that actually forces me to think about a build. This particular boss was one that dodged regular, targeted attacks. As it happens, my entire party was devoid of area-of-effect attacks, so I was somewhat up a creek without a paddle.

## Build options

From the start, Mewgenics has been pretty forgiving on build. Yes, it's possible to get it wrong or get unlucky and you all die, but there have been no hard requirements so far. This was, however, the first time a boss has specifically leant into a weakness in the group.

Not having any AoE attacks was a bnit unusual, as I do tend to spec a ranger into that kind of build, but it meant I was at a loss. I played the battle out, but I knew I was going to lose very, very early. The boss, called something like Dyrrbuk, hopped out of the way of all of my attacks.

To be clear, this is on the whole a good thing. I like being forced to think about what I need to do and plan for possibilities. Even if the first instance is a little frustrating, especially when you lose a team of 'good cats'.

## Directionless?

Although I'm enjoying the general loop of the game, I'm starting to struggle to see where it goes. I don't properly understand what I'm going to be unlocking next. I don't know if I'm working towards the right things. I don't need to be spoonfed this stuff, but I'm struggling to intuit it from the game itself. This isn't technically a "problem", but I'm starting to wonder what the point is, or how I evolve the game and layer in new areas or abilities. This is probably weighing more on my mind than normal as the run length is longer and it therefore takes me several evenings to get through one.

I've adjusted my current rating as a result, as it's something that feels like it is detracting from the experience a little.
