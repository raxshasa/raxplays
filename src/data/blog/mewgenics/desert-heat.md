Something I like about Mewgenics is that you can't just approach situations in the same way, you actually have to think your way around. The desert is a good example of this.

## Healing dramas

I mentioned in the last session that the desert removes the post-battle healing capabilities of your cats. This makes certain aspects of your build even more important than ever. For example, I lost a couple of sets of cats just based on trying the same things as I did from the alley onwards.

Instead, I now need to account for this. I either need to generate more money in order to buy water along the route. Or I need to rely more on traits to gain health back. Or I need to spec a cleric or any class into more healing abilities, or food generation.

At the moment, I'm not doing a good job of it. It's something I need to focus more on when choosing my kitties.
