I feel like I'm still waiting on the meta to fully appear, outside of unlocking new areas, but the core loop is great.

And perhaps I'm being too impatient in calling the meta slow. There are things that are unlocking, such as new locations, and those are fairly meaty. The meta that is slower is around upgrading your base. It's the process of sending cats to the various weirdos that hold the keys to more rooms, more furniture and... other things? I'm not 100% sure everything that they provide.

## Slow is smooth and smooth is fast

I'm not even suggesting the "slow meta" is a bad thing. I don't mind that it takes a bit of time to get certain areas levelled up. It's mostly a case of feeling like it takes a _lot_ of cats to get to the next stage with vendors. I also don't have a huge amount of money, so buying new furniture etc is quite slow.

Again, this isn't bad. It's just a feeling that I can't properly evaluate my options or that I'm doing something wrong. It gives me pause for thought.

There are cool things that are being unlocked. At the end of my last run, which was the first time I finished the sewers, I was told that there were a bunch of moves being added to the pool for various classes. That's neat: it's a system I always liked in Slay the Spire and represents another way to drip feed things to players.

## Loop love

I'm aware the above could all come off as negative. Generally, however, I'm feeling super positive about Mewgenics. The core loop is _great_ and very satisfying. I have mild concerns about run length, but it's easy to save and resume at any time, so those are mitigated by not being forced to abandon things. For a roguelike, it's quite chunky, however. This _could_ bring in more concerns if the game weren't reliably delivering lovely combos etc, but so far that's not come to pass (too often...).

## Next up

I think I'm soon to hit enough cats that I get the first vendor upgrade that I've been working towards. I'm in my first run post unlocked new abilities, so I'm interested to see what appears and how I can best make use of it!
