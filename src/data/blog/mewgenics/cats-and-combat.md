After a couple of tough runs, I was starting to rethink my enthusiasm about crafting builds: there's some RNG to deal with here. But a word on combat...

In short, it's snappy and well put together. Many of the abilities are fun and synergise well with one another. Even if the RNG gods deal you a bad hand, there's _probably_ some tools at your disposable to deal with it, to an extent.

## Combat kitties

I've already noted how much I like the class system here, and that shines in combat. When you lock in your collars, your cats are given random abilities from a specific pool. While, as noted, this can lead to some underwhelming options, it also generates some gold, both in terms of suped up kitties and storytelling.

This is where Nico Robin comes in. Named, apparently, after some manga character, she is my tank in my current run. She came in with decent health stats and I've specced her further to be an absolute machine at taking hits. Enemies prioritise her, her equipment reflects her role. She seems, personality wise, to not give a shit about anything. I love it.

![Behold, Nico Robin](src/assets/images/mewgenics/nico-robin-mewgenics.jpg)
_Behold, Nico Robin_

She knocks enemies back, she eats rocks. What more could you want? I predicate my battles around getting her into the thick of things to pull attention away from my other three cats.

She's backed up my my cleric, Metal, who has a delightful double combo where they is able to create a "holy shield" item, which protects from one instance of damage when picked up, as well as an ability that applies all of their item pickup effects to all cats. So not only is Nico Robin in the middle of it all, armoured up with a wooden bowl on her head a lead shoulder pads, she gets this shield once per turn.

My findings are that this is, on the whole, pretty common. These fun little combos pop up more often than not. I believe I mostly just got unlucky with a couple of runs where nothing like this manifested and all my cats were wiped out.

## Next up

I'm on my first run through the Junkyard, which I like a lot more than the Sewers, and I'm starting to think this title might be an all-timer in the roguelike stakes. I need to see more on the meta-progression front before upping my current rating, but it's looking promising.
