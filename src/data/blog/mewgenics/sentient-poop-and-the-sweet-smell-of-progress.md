Finally, some progression. And I get it. So much unlocked, so much more potential. I'm on the upswing again.

It has taken a while to get there. I have been banging my head against a skill ceiling / progression cap but the dam sure has burst now.

## Unlocks

In one fell swoop I unlocked several possible starting moves for four classes, a whole new class, a whole new biome, a new significant feeling quest and a whole new mechanic for my between run meta. If that sounds like a lot, it's because it is.

![Unlocking so much gave me another chance to see some interactions with the frankly very odd cast of characters in Mewgenics](src/assets/images/mewgenics/cat-grease.jpg)
_Unlocking so much gave me another chance to see some interactions with the frankly very odd cast of characters in Mewgenics_

I was thinking about which of the unlocks I was most interested in. Part of what makes this an exciting event in this title is that it's hard to say. They are all very cool and all make a material difference to how I play. Having done a run with the new class, I'm going to say that that's the thing I'm most interested in, as it introduces some neat changes. It's interesting how the new class uses mechanics that I've come across already, but is able to bundle them thematically to make for an engaging playstyle.

I've also started to come across more of those delightful synergies that I've mentioned previously. As noted, these happen more often than I feel like is true in other titles. For example...

![A poop generating cat that brings poop to life. Of course.](src/assets/images/mewgenics/lotte-poop.jpg)
_A poop generating cat that brings poop to life. Of course._

My kitty here, Lotte, generates poop every time she moves. Destroying that poop creates useful items that everyone can pick up, like health. I therefore added some movement abilities to her. A couple of battles later, I ended up with another item that brought generated poops to life. This meant that I was generating two or three sentient, fighting poops every turn. They weren't super strong - they are just poops after all - but they ended up "flooding the zone" with distractions that caused chip damamge. It was strong and funny all at the same time.

![My poops moving in for the kill](src/assets/images/mewgenics/sentient-poop.jpg)
_My poops moving in for the kill_

All in all, this was one of those delightful combos that was all of effective, interesting to play with a kind of funny.

## Next up

This drop of content has fired up my interest in Mewgenics and I'm looking forward to trying out the new biome next, now that I have more tools at my disposal.
