It has been a hot second since I've played Back 4 Blood, which I tried the beta of way back when. I am a big fan of the Left 4 Dead titles and Back 4 Blood feels like an... OK... sucessor to me.

## Poppin' zombos

Inherently, the game is nice to play. The gunplay is good, movement is good, teamwork elements seem fine etc. I also, on the whole, like the environments. Having said that, the early missions all appear to happen at night time, which makes everything a little dark and samey. From the store page, there are clearly some more daytime based sections, which it would have been nice to see.

Mostly, however, I was left feeling like it's a little generic. As noted, there is no singular part of the game that "feels bad", but the overall design is samey and uninspired. My memory from the beta was that the card and perk system was pretty unique and was clearly something they were going to play in to, but the implementation of it is ultimately confusing. There's notes when "building a deck" that you will get cards in order and choose them accordingly. However, during our playthroughs this didn't seem to be true and they all appeared to be active at once.

I'm not sure if this is mode based, but it's very unclear. The counter corruption system has similar problems. It could inherently be interesting, in the sense that a director is choosing ways to make your life harder in a somewhat targeted manner, but it doesn't quite play out like that. I don't feel like I saw the impact of those cards at all and didn't really understand what I should have expected to be seeing.

Finally, the zombies themselves don't feel super interesting. It's fun to mow them down, but the mutations looks _roughly_ similar to one another, especially the big guys, and are perhaps slightly too easy to take down. It has been some years, but my memory of the "big guys" in L4D is that they were pretty impactful, especially the witch.

## Next steps

With that said, _Back 4 Blood_ is a fun bit of multiplayer action that happens to be cross-play, which puts it firmly in the minority. If nothing else, this was reason enough to pick it up.
