We did another round of shooting zombies. It's so 'OK' that it almost hurts. Back 4 Blood is simply a very forgettable game. All of the environments look kind of the same. All of the enemies look kind of the same. I don't care much about the guns. The shooting itself is nice.

## Playlist coming in clutch

I did pick up an LMG that was intensely satisfying, but otherwise there's a mission that culminates in a jukebox party with zombies that is genuinely good fun. Ultimately, this title is one that allows me to chat to some folks while playing something relatively mindless. If we were playing this "seriously", it wouldn't last us long at all.

Outside of the samey visuals, it's the card system that is a real let-down here for me. It held a lot of promise: you build a deck for which you can unlock new cards as you progress and the "AI Director" plays their own counter cards to mix things up. You played your own in the order you preset, choosing from a few at a time.

For some reason, this has been chucked away and it now appears all of your cards are active all at once. I think? But it's not quite explained and the help text appears to relate to the system as was at launch. It's a mess, honestly, and I try to interact with it as little as possible.

Anyway, we had some fun and a bit of a chat.
