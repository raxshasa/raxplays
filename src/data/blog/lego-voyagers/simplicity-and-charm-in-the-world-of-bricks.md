It's been on my list for a little while, Voyagers has always appealed to me. I was hoping my son would be able to grasp the controls and so far so good. Mostly. He's only six and struggles a little bit with a game pad.

I'm a huge fan of the creative output of Annapurna, and this looks like a super chill and appealing title. Albeit one that I wouldn't play without there being a small person involved.

## Charm

It's hard to overstate how lovely the presentation of voyagers is. Early on you are introduced to a button that does almost nothing except cause your brick to make a cute little noise. It's properly adorable. I think the function is intended to be that you can find where the brick is if it gets hidden (some of the palette can be hard to parse and the readability shader is medium effective) but mostly we spammed it to make cute sounds.

Otherwise, the set up is pretty straightforward. You each take on the role of a "one-er", to use our own house LEGO parlance. This is a single brick with an eye. There is no speaking, no on screen text, nothing. The story _appears_ to be that a rocket has taken off from a faraway island and fallen to pieces over your home area. You can follow the trail of bits to... I don't know. Narratively, I have no idea why we're following the trail. But it's nice. The world is simply laid out and there are light puzzles / dexterity sections to navigate. At the moment, there's nothing too challenging and it has mostly been an exercise in teaching my son multi-input and timing requirements. 

## Next up

I'm sure we'll carry this on. I'm looking forward to seeing where it goes. I don't imagine it's a particularly long game!
