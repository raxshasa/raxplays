I got this title for free the other day and finally got around to installing it. It's early days, but there's some nice vibes. It's also basically Rimworld, or feels a lot like it.

Yes it's isometric rather than 2D and has some kind of voxel-esque system going on, but otherwise the job, management and build menus might have different UI, but the content is incredibly famiilair.

## Going Rimworld

And that's probably not a bad thing. I had a big thing for Rimworld last year for a time and it remains one of my facourite games of all time. This feels like it does things with the fact that it introduces another dimension that titles such as Stranded: Alien Dawn didn't.

As an example, you can dig underground in order to build cooler storage. You can build towers with merlons for increased range in your archers, as well as more cover. That's neat. As it stands, I'm in the early game and it's therefore the typical slightly slower fare. I'm sure there's plenty to unlock that will make things more interesting.

I will say that the research approach feels interesting. You create physical research - books initially - which are then allocated to nodes on the tree. If those books are somehow lost, you need to build your stock back up again to continue research. Seems neat. We'll see how it goes.
