This is hardly a new one on my, I've completed the game twice, but I definitely have unfinished business to attend to. So, in a moment of weakness, I fired it up again.

## Difficult times
The reason I have unfinished business is the save I started almost two years ago. 

My first two runs were morally good followed by morally grey. For my third one I decided to try what felt like a whole new game mode and do the "Dark Urge" character.

Baldur's Gate is a mind-bogglingly large title with a lot to do and the Dark Urge (or "Durge") changes things even further. Dedciated cutscenes, dialogue, options and even - I suspect - quite large changes to the story. You can shoot for redemption for your Durge or, like me, lean in to the evil.

And boy is it evil.

But that is for another time. The other aspect of my unfinished business is the difficulty. I want to finish the game on Tactician, the games hardest option. 

And boy is it hard. 

I'm stuck on the final boss of Act 2 and have been for two years. It's true that I've not been playing it solidly in that time, or in fact at all, but it's the thing that made me put it down in the first place. I feel like I've built myself into a corner with my characters and would need to backtrack quite a lot to be able to respec and try again. So instead I'm throwing myself at the problem time and time again.

I've probably run this fight almost 50 times and on none of those have I felt particularly close to taking a win.

## Delightful frustration

What made me think about writing this, however, is how surprisingly serene I am about it. Perhaps it's because I've beated the game already, but the sheer _fun_ of the thing, even in the face of repeated defeats, has been great. I'm looking at new ways to apporach things, different options I've never considered and considering my positioning far more than I ever have.

For me, this is what difficulty _should_ mean. The boss has a few more bells and whistles to him, which change attack patterns and how you need to operate. So while there are also a few buffs here and there, these changes to the character were the difference between a medium challenge and one that I am thinking might be insurmountable for me.

## Next up

I want to continue trying to find ways of beating this guy and might enlist the help of someone who is far better at this stuff than I am to join my game for a couple of hours...
