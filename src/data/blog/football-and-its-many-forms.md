I did not get into Rocket League when it first made its entrance on to the scene. It looked like chaotic nonsense that I'd be rubbish at. Turns out that that's true, but it's also big fun.

## Football multiplayer chaos

There's a few of us that try to get together at least once a month to play something. We're across various platforms, from PS5 to PC, and there's actually not that much out there that does good cross play at higher player counts that we all own. Rocket League being free-to-play makes it a dream.

It's a fun game. I'm sure as (if?) you get good at it, there's actually quite a lot of nuance and technique to bear in mind. I'm certianly not involved at that level. I like how fast paced it is, how clearly light strategy and tactics emerge and how forgiving it is when playing casually across skill gaps.

We're able to chat about whatever while stringing some half-decent results together. it's flashy and bright and helps get the endorphings going.

The fact that it's mechanically quite tight makes it easy to understand and play.

## More football for dessert

When we play as a group, it's not uncommon for people to drift a way a little early, especially as we're old and tired. I therefore dipped into Football Manager for a bit before signing off. I made a conscious effort to take a little more time between matches and make use of some new screens that I've otherwise ignored. I think it works. I feel less aggrieved about some of the perceived lack of depth.

I'll continue with the approach, as I've not actually played too much while not distracted by other things, which itself might well be part of the problem.

I did however draw 0-0 with Boston Utd in the end, which is a game where we had chances to win comfrotably, so that pissed me off.
