There's something about this title that feels harder to me. The difficulty in FM24 and older is quite flip-floppy, but I would tend to find that the player was ultimately favoured, even if a run of results went against you. I don't get the same sense here. There's something there that feels less forgiving, and I like it.

## Desperate defending

I've not set the team up to park the bus while out of possession, but the bus probably is driving very slowly. I'm frustrating teams and aiming for smash and grab goals where I can. It's fun, I'm really enjoying it. It's working... some of the time. And as noted in the intro, I feel like games of old would have allowed this to work quite a lot of the time, probably driving me up the table. As it is I'm taking roughly 1.5 points a game, which I think is great, without it feeling like I'm cheating somehow.

It genuinely feels like I can't run a high tempo gegenpress, as my players don't have the engine for it, so I'm favouring more patient and humble approaches.

Additionally, I feel like I can actually change the picture of what's on the pitch. I was seeing opposition getting far too much space in the first 15 metres of my own half, and teams playing direct were delivering devestating balls to their forwards. I've pushed my line of engagement up, while still maintaining a deeper defence, and I feel like I can _actually see_ that space being filled and taken away from my opposition. I don't think that's something that I've ever actually seen in previous titles.

## Next up

I feel like the above compels me to up my rating here. It's new, it's fresh and it feels _great_. However I suspect this is the ceiling for Football Manager 26. The UI, unless made far more intuitive very quickly, is still a huge detractor. I very much hope that the 27 installment unlocks a lot of the promise here.
