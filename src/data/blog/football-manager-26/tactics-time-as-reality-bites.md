Just a quick blast before bed, I got three games played.

## Conservatism is king

I should have known that my opening day victory against Notts County would be a one-off.

I got battered in my next two matches - one of them against League One opposition in the cup, to be fair - and it was clear that my postive-looking, possesion based, entertainment oriented brand of football wasn't quite going to cut it in League Two. My opponents are better in many, many ways, and my attack can't make up for shipping seven goals in two matches.

It got me back to the tactics systems for the first meanginful time since I set up my last formation. I've opted for a far more restrictive and disciplined catenaccio style game, trying to choke wins out. As part of that, I've reverted to my favoured back 5, with wing backs and wide centres pushing into the middle of the park.

And while there are still some odd UI things, this is a process that works well. You can click on entire areas of the park and set up how your team operates in a mid-block, for instance. Things are well explained. While some of the micro UI is baffling _before_ you've been through the options, it's then helpful once you know what you're looking at.

And setting roles for in possesion and out of possesion is equally nice and surprisingly intuitive, given it's a medium complex system even without some tough UI.

## Next steps

All in all, it was good to get back to this as a system and I enjoyed the session. It helped that I then won my first game using the new setup. I felt like I could see it on the pitch, too. It's a process that the series has typically done well and the process of making smaller tweaks over the next few weeks and seeing that bear out on the pitch is something I'm looking forward to.
