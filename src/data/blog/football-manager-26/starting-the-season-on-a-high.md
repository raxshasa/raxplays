Pre-season in Football Manager is always odd: the first half of it I find incredibly boring and the last half into the new season is one of my favourite times in the game. This is my first "between seasons" pre-season in the new edition and it lived up to expectations.

## Free transfer

As Woking I'm not going to be splashing the cash for quite a while, if ever, so I'm on the hunt for good freebies and PL youngsters on loan. The game... still fights this. I find setting up the scouting focuses quite tedious and clunky and I don't feel like it offers what I'm after. Really, I wanted my scouts looking at PL U18 sides to find someone, but I don't seem to be able to specify that. If I in fact can, it's well buried.

This does somewhat dent my enthusiasm. With that said, I did end up making some signings I was super happy with, which was great.

Another negative was that I, very suddenly, fell foul of the FFP regulations for League Two, which seemed suspiciously harsh. I don't think it's right that clubs can only spend £35k a week on wages: I need to go and look up what is actually happening. I ended up briefly under embargo while I shipped some of the boys out on load.

Those gripes aside, I've enjoyed getting back into the squad building side of things and seeing how the new signings gelled in the friendlies. I took the chance to give some of the youth a chance in the rotation, too, and it's another aspect of the series I love coming to the fore. Youth development. After my golden generation, I'm looking forward to seeing how it goes.

## New skins

I played one single competitive game in the new season against Notts County, a side I'd expect to struggle against. Since the last time I fired up FM there has been a patch that improves squad selection. It's still bafflingly hard to have right clicks actually register, but at least it's now much, much easier to _actually choose players_, which is most welcome. It makes the whole process feel a lot sleeker.

When starting the match, it was cool to see the League Two branding in play for the intro slides as well as for the scoreboard and match timer. This is a great way to make the experience of the matches feel distinct based on the type of fixture you're playing. It's something that just doesn't pop up in the national leagues.

## Next up

My approach of "trying to like the game" is still working, even if the sluggishness and layout of some screens is still a bit of a disaster. There are signs of life in each patch and I was excited to fire the game up and enjoyed the time I spent with it. There's just too much still not quite clicking to feel like it deserves a bump to my opinion of it.

I beat Notts County 3-1, which did help a lot though.
