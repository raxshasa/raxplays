Howdy, I'm raxshasa. This is a personal blog about games I play. I'm a game dev based in the UK and having two kids means I'm not able to spend as much time playing games as I used to. This site is here to catalogue my thoughts in a small way, which might sometimes even ladder up to a review \**gasp\**

I play most genres, but you can expect to hear plenty about [simulation](../../tags/simulation), [management](../../tags/management) and [city builder](../../tags/city-builder) games in particular. Sorry about that.
## Platforms
As a man of gaming, there are a few ways I like to consume games.
### PC
This is the main one, a good chuck of my time is gaming on PC. I have a modest rig, which will likely need upgrading at some point in the not too distant, but it does the job. Many of the games I play are not super new, so that mitigates the issue.
#### Specs
- CPU: Ryzen 3600 X
- GPU: NVIDIA 2070 SUPER
- RAM: 64GB

### Steam Deck
The Steam Deck has been a great purchase for me. While I hide away in my office a couple of nights a week to play games, the Steam Deck has meant I can play on the sofa, too. I very rarely use it in docked mode with roughly 95% of my playtime being handheld. There are some titles I wouldn't touch on Deck: mostly the heavier UI titles or more detailed building games.

[Check out my Steam Deck posts](../../tags/steamdeck)

### Analgue 3D
I'm not a retro nerd by any means, but the Analogue 3D tickled a big nostalgia itch for me. Combined with the fact it's fuss free and the hardware looked lovely, I was sold. I'll basically always refer to it as the "N64" rather than the Analogue 3D, because in reality I'm 13 years old.
